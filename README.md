About
OS7 Plus! is a remake of my MD submission OS7 in MD.  Play as Subject 7, an unnamed quality assurance tester for the latest and greatest computer operating system, Optimal-System 7! However, things may not be as they seem once you break free from the monotonous quality assurance chamber.

Improved environments
OS7 Plus! has improved upon the blocky, primitive environments of the MD version with new and improved maps which contain several new details for you to find.

Improved endings
While several new endings are planned for Plus!, the endings from the 7DFPS release of OS7 are also getting revamped with updated visuals and recontextualized plot points.

More story
Optimal PC has a complicated history and in OS7 Plus!, you will uncover the pieces of this corporation's history and maybe discover something unbelievable later on. Existing narratives will be expanded upon with some loose ends being tied up while new ends are loosened.

What about the 7dfps version?
The 7dfps 2022 version is still available and will always be. If you wish to check out the 7dfps version before the release of OS7 Plus!, you can do so with this link: https://sasha191261creates.itch.io/os7-plus
